Mesh size is one of the most critical yet misunderstood specifications in activated carbon selection. The wrong particle size can lead to excessive pressure drop, bypassing, or poor contact time. This guide explains the fundamentals.

## What is Mesh Size?

Mesh size refers to the number of openings per linear inch in a screen. Higher mesh numbers mean smaller particles:

- **8x30 mesh** = particles pass through 8-mesh screen but retained on 30-mesh
- **12x40 mesh** = finer particles, more surface area per volume
- **4x8 mesh** = coarser particles, lower pressure drop

## Selecting Mesh Size by Application

### Water Treatment (GAC)
- **Municipal drinking water**: 12x40 or 8x30 mesh
- **Industrial wastewater**: 8x30 or 12x40 mesh
- **Point-of-use filters**: 12x40 mesh for better contact

### Air & Gas Treatment
- **VOC removal**: 4x8 or 6x12 mesh pellets
- **Gas-phase odor control**: 4x10 mesh
- **Solvent recovery**: 4x8 mesh for low pressure drop

### Gold Recovery (CIP/CIL)
- **Carbon-in-pulp**: 6x12 or 6x16 mesh
- **Carbon-in-leach**: 6x12 mesh (balance attrition vs. screening)

## Pressure Drop Considerations

Finer mesh = higher pressure drop. For gravity filters and air systems, balance contact time against head loss:

| Mesh Size | Pressure Drop | Contact Time |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| 4x8       | Low           | Shorter      |
| 8x30      | Medium        | Medium       |
| 12x40     | High          | Longer       |

## Custom Sizing Available

CarboPure can provide any mesh range between 4x100 mesh. Common custom requests:
- 6x12 mesh for specific CIP circuits
- 20x50 mesh for ultra-fine filtration
- 4x10 mesh for low-pressure industrial air

## Ordering Tip

Always specify both the coarse and fine mesh (e.g., "8x30 mesh") rather than a single number. This ensures you receive particles within the intended size distribution.
