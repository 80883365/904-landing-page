Activated carbon plays a critical role in pharmaceutical manufacturing, from API synthesis to final formulation. Purity standards are stringent, and the right carbon selection directly impacts product quality.

## Primary Applications in Pharma

### 1. API Decolorization
Removing color bodies and impurities from active pharmaceutical ingredients:
- **Carbon type**: Powdered activated carbon (PAC), 200+ mesh
- **Dosage**: 0.5-5% by weight of crude API
- **Contact time**: 30-90 minutes with agitation
- **Filtration**: Pressure or vacuum through filter press

### 2. Solvent Recovery
Recovering expensive organic solvents (ethanol, toluene, DCM):
- **Carbon type**: Pelletized or GAC in fixed-bed adsorbers
- **Regeneration**: Steam desorption at 120-150掳C
- **Recovery rate**: 95-99% of adsorbed solvent
- **Cycle time**: 4-8 hours adsorption, 2-4 hours regeneration

### 3. Process Water Treatment
Removing organics and chlorine from purified water systems:
- **Carbon type**: GAC 12x40 mesh, USP/EP compliant
- **Application**: Pre-treatment for RO or post-DI polishing
- **Replacement**: Every 12-18 months or per validation protocol

### 4. Air Purification (Cleanrooms)
VOC and odor removal from HVAC systems:
- **Carbon type**: Impregnated pellets (KOH or KI)
- **Application**: In-duct modules or standalone scrubbers
- **Media life**: 12-24 months depending on loading

## Regulatory Compliance

### USP/EP Grade Carbon
Pharmaceutical-grade carbon must meet:
- **Heavy metals**: <10 ppm total, <1 ppm lead
- **Sulfated ash**: <5%
- **pH**: 5-10 (aqueous slurry)
- **Microbiological**: <100 CFU/g total count

### Documentation Requirements
- **Certificate of Analysis** (CoA) for every batch
- **Manufacturing lot traceability**
- **Material Safety Data Sheet** (SDS)
- **Drug Master File** (DMF) reference for audits

## Case Study: Antibiotic Decolorization

A manufacturer producing a beta-lactam antibiotic faced color contamination in the crystallization step:

**Problem**:
- Yellow-brown discoloration in final API
- Impurities at 0.3% (above 0.1% spec)

**Solution**:
- **Carbon**: Coconut-shell PAC, 325 mesh, acid-washed

## Selecting Carbon for Pharma

### For Decolorization (PAC)
- High surface area (>1000 m虏/g)
- Fine mesh (200-400)
- Acid-washed to remove impurities
- Low extractables (<0.5% on ignition)

### For Solvent Recovery (Pellet/GAC)
- High micropore volume for organics
- Crush strength >95% (pellets)
- Low dust (<1%)
- Regenerable without degradation

### For Water Polishing (GAC)
- NSF-certified or USP-grade
- Low leachables (TOC, UV254)
- 12x40 mesh for standard filters
- Virgin (not reactivated)
