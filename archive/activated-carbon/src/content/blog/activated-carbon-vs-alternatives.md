## When to Use Activated Carbon

### Ideal Applications
- **Organic removal**: Pesticides, herbicides, industrial chemicals
- **Chlorine/chloramine**: Drinking water dechlorination
- **Taste & odor**: Geosmin, MIB, algal metabolites
- **VOC control**: Industrial emissions, solvent recovery
- **Color removal**: Food, beverage, chemical decolorization

### Strengths
- Broad-spectrum organics removal
- Effective at low concentrations (ppb-ppm range)
- NSF-certified grades for drinking water
- Regenerable (thermal reactivation)

### Limitations
- **Poor for inorganics**: Salts, hardness, nitrate not removed
- **Biofouling**: Can harbor bacteria (need chlorine residual)
- **Fines generation**: Abrasion in high-flow systems
- **Not selective**: Removes beneficial organics along with targets

### Activated Alumina

**Best for**:
- Fluoride removal (drinking water defluoridation)
- Arsenic (V) removal (oxidized arsenic)

### Ion Exchange Resins

**Best for**:
- Hardness removal (Ca虏鈦? Mg虏鈦?softening)
- Nitrate/nitrite removal (anion exchange)
- Heavy metal removal (Cu, Pb, Ni, Zn)
- Demineralization (ultrapure water production)

**Advantages**:
- Extremely high selectivity (ion-specific resins available)
- High capacity (>2 eq/L resin)
- Regenerable 100s of times (brine or acid/caustic)
- Fast kinetics (minutes vs. hours for carbon)

**Limitations**:
- High capital cost (resin + regeneration system)
- Brine disposal (environmental/regulatory challenge)
- Fouling from organics, iron, sulfate
- NOT effective for organics or uncharged species

**Typical Use Case**: Industrial boiler feedwater softening, preventing scale formation.

## When in Doubt, Pilot Test

For complex water or air streams, bench-scale or pilot testing is essential:
- **Obtain actual loading data** (not just isotherm estimates)
- **Test regeneration effectiveness** (cycles to breakthrough)
- **Evaluate fouling** (particulates, biofilm, scaling)
- **Compare TCO** (media, regeneration, disposal, labor)
