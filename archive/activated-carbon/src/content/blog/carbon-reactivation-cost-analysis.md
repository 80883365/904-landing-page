## Understanding Thermal Reactivation

### The Process
1. **High-temperature heating** (800-900掳C) in controlled atmosphere
2. **Combustion of adsorbed organics** and thermal desorption
3. **Pore structure restoration** through controlled oxidation
4. **Cooling and reactivation** of surface chemistry

### Capacity Recovery
- **First cycle**: 90-95% of virgin capacity
- **Second cycle**: 85-90%
- **Third+ cycles**: 80-85%
- **Structural losses**: 5-10% per cycle (attrition, fines generation)

## When Reactivation Makes Sense

### Ideal Candidates
1. **Large volumes** (>5 tons/year spent carbon)
2. **Organic contaminants** only (no heavy metals, PFAS, or radioactive materials)
3. **Consistent carbon type** (same grade and mesh size)
4. **Regular replacement cycles** (predictable generation rate)

### Best Applications
- **Solvent recovery** (clean organic loading)
- **Wastewater treatment** (biodegradable organics)
- **Food & beverage** (color, taste, odor removal)
- **Groundwater remediation** (petroleum hydrocarbons)

## When to Replace with Virgin Carbon

### Reactivation NOT Recommended For:
1. **Heavy metal contamination** (Cr, Pb, Hg, As)
2. **PFAS-laden carbon** (requires incineration >1000掳C)
3. **Radioactive or hazmat** waste carbon
4. **Low-value applications** where replacement cost < reactivation + logistics
5. **Small volumes** (<1 ton/year: reactivation minimums not met)

### Application-Specific Considerations
- **Gold recovery CIP/CIL**: Reactivate only if gold values are recovered first
- **Pharmaceutical**: Virgin carbon often mandated by validation protocols
- **Drinking water**: Virgin preferred for regulatory compliance

## Logistics and Turnaround

### Off-Site Reactivation
- **Packaging**: Return in super sacks or bulk truck
- **Transport time**: 1-2 weeks
- **Reactivation cycle**: 2-3 weeks
- **Total turnaround**: 4-6 weeks
- **Plan**: Requires backup inventory or staged replacement

### On-Site Reactivation (Mobile Units)
- **Minimum volume**: 50+ tons/year
- **Equipment**: Portable rotary kiln or fluidized bed
- **Turnaround**: Days instead of weeks
- **Cost**: Higher $/ton but no transport
