## Ball milling engineered around your gold ore

A gold ore ball mill reduces crushed ore to the particle size needed for downstream gravity concentration, flotation or leaching. The correct mill is selected from real process inputs—not diameter alone. Ore hardness, feed size distribution, target P80, circulating load and available power all affect the final configuration.

Our engineering team uses those inputs to recommend the mill type, liner arrangement, grinding media charge and auxiliary equipment required for a stable grinding circuit.

## Where a ball mill fits in the process

A typical hard-rock circuit is **crushing → screening → ball milling → classification → recovery**. Ball mills are best suited to continuous operations that need finer, more controlled grinding than a small wet pan mill can provide.

### Selection data we need

- Ore type and Bond Work Index, if available
- Maximum feed size and target product size
- Required dry-solids throughput in tonnes per hour
- Wet or dry process and water availability
- Downstream recovery method
- Site voltage, frequency and altitude

## Key design options

Choose overflow discharge for simpler, fine-grinding duties or grate discharge where faster slurry removal and higher throughput are priorities. Rubber, high-manganese steel and alloy liners can be matched to abrasion, impact and maintenance requirements. The drive package can be adapted to the site's power supply and operating conditions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What capacity can a gold ore ball mill handle?

Capacity varies widely with mill dimensions, ore hardness, feed size and target fineness. Send a representative ore analysis and required throughput for model selection.

### Which grinding balls should I use?

Forged steel balls are commonly selected for demanding wet mineral grinding because they balance hardness and impact toughness. Ball diameter and grade should be matched to mill size and ore characteristics.

### Can the mill be supplied as part of a complete line?

Yes. A proposal can include feeding, crushing, classification, pumps, conveyors and downstream gravity equipment as required.
