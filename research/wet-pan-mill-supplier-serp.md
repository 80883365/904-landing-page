Target market/language: international B2B buyers searching in English

## Keyword and intent

Primary keyword: `wet pan mill supplier`

Commercial variants reviewed:

- `wet pan mill manufacturer`
- `gold wet pan mill for sale`
- `wet pan mill price`
- `1200 wet pan mill`
- `1500 wet pan mill for gold`
- `diesel wet pan mill`
- `wet pan mill for gold ore`

The results are predominantly supplier product pages and B2B marketplace listings. This is bottom-of-funnel commercial investigation: buyers want a model, credible specification basis, price/packing scope and reassurance that the supplier understands installation conditions.

## Gaps and conversion strategy

Common pages lead with a catalogue description or a broad low-price claim. The landing page instead reduces purchase uncertainty:

1. **Attention:** exact keyword, gold-ore use case and the 1100–1600 range.
2. **Interest:** explains when the mill fits a small plant and which site inputs matter.
3. **Desire:** model comparison, real operating imagery, equipment-scope guidance and a quote checklist.
4. **Action:** repeated modal CTAs asking for four high-value facts—ore, feed size, throughput and power—without adding prohibited form fields.

## Claims policy

- Public supplier specifications conflict across sites and configurations. All table figures are explicitly preliminary until confirmed by an approved factory datasheet.
- The page does not promise a gold-recovery percentage, because recovery depends on liberation, mineralogy and downstream separation.
- The page does not use unverifiable “lowest price,” “best quality,” global shipment, customer count or response-time claims.
- The final commercial version needs confirmed company identity, domain/contact details, Web3Forms key, factory data and licensed/original photographs.
