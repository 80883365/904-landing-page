## SERP pattern

Manufacturer product pages dominate all three core clusters. Wet pan mill results reward detailed model tables and practical small-mine process context. Ball mill results combine technical product pages with authoritative engineering resources. Grinding ball results favor category depth, diameter range, hardness, alloy, heat treatment and quality-control evidence. The SERP is commercial, with an informational layer that helps buyers select before asking for a quote.

## Recommended page strategy

Use one focused product page per product family, supported by a category page and a solution-led homepage. Each page should answer fit, technical range, selection inputs, process placement, delivery scope and FAQ before the modal inquiry CTA. Avoid a single thin three-product page and avoid splitting every model into a separate URL until unique model data and photography are available.

## Existing page decision

The former activated-carbon pages did not match the new market or keywords, so replacement pages were created at new descriptive URLs. The homepage and navigation now link directly to all three pages. Old blog content is no longer linked in primary navigation and should be replaced with mining-specific guides before the new domain is launched.
