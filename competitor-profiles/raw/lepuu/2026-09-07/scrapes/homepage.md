# LIPU wet pan mill page — raw notes

URL: https://www.lepuu.com/wet-pan-mill/

Public page states a typical feed below 20–30 mm, output below 1 mm, model range 1100–1600, nominal capacity 0.5–3 t/h, installed power 5.5–37 kW and speed 11–22 rpm. It positions the machine for small rock-gold circuits and describes a crusher → wet pan mill → gravity concentration flow.
